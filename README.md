SOLMARINA — an original top-down open-world crime game for make.fun gta6-01: a sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car, traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a 5-star wanted level with police who respond, 6 story missions from fixer Ada Kline, combat, a HUD with a live minimap, a start screen and saving. Vanilla JS on canvas, no libraries, no network — it plays fully offline in a desktop browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and press Enter.

## Controls

- **WASD / arrows** — move & drive · **Shift** — sprint
- **Mouse** — aim · **Click / F** — punch & shoot
- **E** — steal / enter a car, respray at the spray shop, take Ada's next job
- **Space** — handbrake · **1 / 2 / 3** — fists / pistol / SMG
- **P** — save · **M** — radio on/off · **Esc** — pause

## The city

Solmarina is a fully original city: Palmera Park, Solmarina Pier, East Harbour, Casino Lumen, Mama Rosa's Diner, Rosa's Garage, the Sunset Spray Shop, Solmarina General hospital and a safehouse on the west side. No Rockstar / GTA names, characters, art, music or maps — everything here is invented for this game.
